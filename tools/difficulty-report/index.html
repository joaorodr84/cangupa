Difficulty is pathBits: the luck a blind player needs to retrace one solution, the sum of log₂(legal moves) over its steps. Exact bits is −log₂ of the chance that uniform random play clears the level, computed over every reachable position; it is blank where the level has too many positions to enumerate. Decisions counts steps with more than one legal move. Effort is how many positions a depth-first solver expands before it finds a solution. Random play is the average share of the level a random player completes before getting stuck. Definitions live in tools/difficulty.js.
