Cangupa visualizers

Every page under tools/visualizers/. Hand-authored review pages are design comparisons, built to look at before a change lands in Board.tsx or palette.ts — nothing here is loaded by the app itself. The generated pages are a straight rendering of tools/packs.json, rebuilt by gen-visualizers.js whenever that file or its own template changes.

Design reviews

Down Under tiles tile-down-under.html Candidate looks for the three tiles the Down Under collection adds — Pillar, Mud and Tunnel — with a board that redraws from the picks, on both pits and at three sizes (CANGUPA-158). Tile outlines tile-outlines.html Legal-move vs. hint outline, across all 5 tile kinds and both themes — width and border-style, not colour, tell the two apart (CANGUPA-126). Joey arm tuner kangaroo-joey-arm.html Live sliders for the parent kangaroo's forearm and the joey's forward lean in the side-facing sprite, so the arm stops starting inside the baby's head (CANGUPA-128). Tile marks tile-marks.html Departure/Arrival edge-mark design comparison: the shipped triangle against the doorway-bracket alternative, across all 4 directions. Tile metals tile-metals.html Tile colour and visit-mark design comparison (bars, numbers, dice dots, diagonal strokes), plus a board mock with colour-vision and low-vision filters. Rules rules.html A rules reference for apps/app: step, jump, every tile kind and every board button, rendered with the game's real pieces and icons.
