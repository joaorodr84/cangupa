Option B

Doorway bracket

The actual exit/enter pictogram, threaded onto the tile's edge: a bracket open on the side facing the seam, shut on the other three. Departure's arrow starts inside the bracket and threads all the way out through the opening, tip past the edge. Arrival's starts outside, at the edge, and threads in through the same opening, tip arriving near the bracket's closed back wall — pulled back a little so there's daylight between the arrow and the wall, not touching it. Drawn large and recentred on the tile, with equal padding on all four sides, rather than tucked against its edge.
