Difficulty is pathBits: the luck a blind player needs to retrace one solution, the sum of log₂(legal moves) over its steps. Decisions counts steps with more than one legal move. Effort is how many positions a depth-first solver expands before it finds a solution. Definitions live in tools/difficulty.js.
