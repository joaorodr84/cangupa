Cangupa app — rules reference

How to play

Step on every tile on the board until none are left. You start on the tile marked with the kangaroo. The diagrams below elaborate on the app's own in-game Rules screen (HelpScreen.tsx) with real board pieces instead of words alone.

Moves

Step

Solid outline: one step away.

Move one tile up, down, left or right.

Jump, over a gap

Dashed outline: the far tile, two away in a line.

The middle tile is already gone — you jump straight over the empty space.

Jump, over a tile

The middle tile is untouched by the jump.

The middle tile is still there, but a jump skips it — it isn't visited and doesn't lose a visit. Only the highlighted tile at the far end counts.

You can only land on a tile that still has visits left. The moment you leave a tile, it disappears — there's no stepping back onto it, only undo.

When you're stuck

If no legal move remains, that's a dead end: undo a few moves or restart the level. Nothing is ever permanently lost to a bad path — undo and restart are always there.

Buttons

Between Back and this group, the board screen also shows the level number and move count — replaced by "Dead end. Undo a few moves." or "No moves left. Undo or restart." whenever one applies.
