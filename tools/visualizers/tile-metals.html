Candidate names and colours for tiles that need more than one visit. Each tile is drawn on the game's real background, light and dark, so you can judge how it reads in play.

Visit marks: bars

One bar per visit left, like the gold tile today. Each tile is shown as the visits run down, left to right. The gold row marked “as in the game today” uses the game's own mark, 28% black, which measures about 1.9:1 against the gold. The other rows use a colour that holds at least 4.7:1.

Visit marks: numbers

The digit is the number of visits left. Same tiles and colours as above.

Visit marks: dice dots

Dots in the layout of a die: one in the centre, two on a diagonal, three on a diagonal. A small count like this is read at a glance, without counting. The Common tile is shown with one dot to see whether marking it too helps; today it has no mark.

A big board on a small screen

An 8×7 board (the largest pack) at phone width, about 41 pixels per cell, with all three tiles and the four mark styles side by side. The tiles show visits left, so some are partly used. The kangaroo stands on the start tile, a Steel with two visits left, and covers its marks. Use the controls to try the dark theme and to see the board as people with colour blindness or low vision might.

Visit marks: diagonal strokes

Short strokes from top right to bottom left, one per visit left, side by side like tally marks. Same tiles and colours as the other examples. The Common tile is shown with one stroke to see whether marking it too helps; today it has no mark.

Arrow tile

A Common tile you can only leave in one direction, steps and jumps alike. The final design: a solid triangle tip on a thicker shaft, a raspberry tile and a white arrow. Shown in the four directions (right, down, left, up), on both game backgrounds, then again at board size (about 36 pixels). The second card is the edge-triangle version: the triangle sits on the edge you leave by.

Backgrounds are the game's own, from the themes package's background: light #eef0f4, dark #0c0d10. They stay the same whichever theme this page is in. The board the tiles sit on is a little different: #d9dee6 (light) and #1c1f26 (dark), inside a frame of #ffffff and #15171c. The common tile and the gold tile are the game's current colours (#4cc9f0 and #ffc857). Rows run from the most visits to the fewest.
